## When to use this skill

To write internal communications, use this skill for:
- 3P updates (Progress, Plans, Problems)
- Company newsletters
- FAQ responses
- Status reports
- Leadership updates
- Project updates
- Incident reports

## Communication Types

### 3P Updates (Progress, Plans, Problems)

**Format:**
```
🏢 [Team Name] — [Start Date] to [End Date]

📈 Progress
• [Achievement with data/metrics - 1-3 sentences max]
• [Achievement with data/metrics - 1-3 sentences max]

📋 Plans
• [What's coming next - 1-3 sentences max]
• [What's coming next - 1-3 sentences max]

⚠️ Problems
• [Blocker or risk - 1-3 sentences max]
• [Blocker or risk - 1-3 sentences max]
```

**Guidelines:**
- 1-3 sentences per bullet point
- Data-driven (include numbers, metrics, percentages)
- 30-60 second read time
- Focus on what leadership needs to know
- Be honest about problems - don't sugarcoat

### Company Newsletter

**Format:**
- ~20-25 bullet points total
- Broken into sections (Product, Engineering, People, etc.)
- Uses "we" tense throughout
- Links to sources (Slack threads, docs, emails)

**Guidelines:**
- Keep each bullet to 1-2 sentences
- Lead with the most important news
- Balance celebrations with real updates
- Include cross-team highlights

### FAQ Responses

**Format:**
```
Q: [Question - 1 sentence]
A: [Answer - 1-2 sentences]
```

**Guidelines:**
- Questions should be things people actually ask
- Answers should be direct and actionable
- Link to detailed docs when available
- Cover the whole company, not just one team

### General Communications

For communications not matching the above formats:

1. **Identify the audience** - Who needs to read this?
2. **Define the purpose** - What action should they take?
3. **Set the tone** - Formal, casual, urgent?
4. **Choose the format** - Email, Slack, document?

**Universal guidelines:**
- Lead with the key message
- Keep paragraphs short (2-3 sentences)
- Use bullet points for lists
- End with clear next steps or call to action
- Proofread for tone and clarity
